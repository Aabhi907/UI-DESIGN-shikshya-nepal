## Research rules

- Work on **one institution per response**. Never merge facts from similarly named institutions.
- Prefer the institution's current official website, current prospectus/fee notice, university or regulator pages, and official admissions notices.
- Record a direct URL for every factual claim. If a fact has no reliable source, return `unknown`.
- Do not use search snippets, directories, social posts, reviews, rankings, marketing claims, or another AI answer as evidence.
- Treat programmes, affiliations, fees, seats, admission dates, scholarships and outcomes as time-sensitive. Include the academic year/intake and date checked.
- An awarding body or affiliation must be stated **per programme** when programmes differ. Do not generalise an affiliation from one course to the entire institution.
- Do not infer fee totals from a monthly/semester figure. Record the stated payment period and every stated mandatory one-time charge separately.
- Do not write “best”, “top”, “leading”, placement rates, pass rates, rankings, awards or outcome claims unless an official source supplies the exact claim and date.
- Keep school-level institutions (ECD–Grade 10) in the school taxonomy; keep +2, diploma, Bachelor, Master and higher study in the college taxonomy.

```text
Use web research. Prefer official institution pages, official university/regulator pages, current prospectuses, and current official admission or fee notices. Do not use search-result snippets, directories, social posts, reviews, rankings, marketing claims, or unsupported AI text as sources.

Rules:
1. Never guess. Write "unknown" when an official source does not support a field.
2. Put a direct source URL beside every fact and state "checked on: [today's date]".
3. Treat fees, intake dates, seats, scholarships, programme lists and affiliations as time-sensitive.
4. Confirm affiliation/awarding body separately for every programme; do not apply one course's affiliation to all courses.
5. Separate a fee amount, payment period, academic year/intake, and one-time charges. Never calculate a total unless the official source explicitly publishes it.
6. Flag contradictions, old notices, unclear campus locations, duplicate names, and school-versus-college taxonomy problems.
7. Do not give an overall-quality score, ranking, or “best college” claim.

Return exactly these sections:

1. Identity and taxonomy
   - official legal/display name
   - type: school / +2 college / technical-diploma provider / higher-education college / university constituent school / other
   - current status: open / closed / unclear
   - exact campus name(s), address, municipality, district, province
   - official website, phone, email, map/directions URL

2. Recognition and awarding bodies
   - institution-wide recognition only when officially stated
   - programme-by-programme awarding body, affiliation and regulator approval where published

3. Current programmes
   For every programme: official name, level, duration, study mode, intake, awarding body/affiliation, eligibility, entrance requirement, source URL, checked date.

4. Fees, seats and scholarships
   For every programme with evidence: amount in NPR, payment period, academic year/intake, fee components, seats, scholarship/waiver terms, source URL, checked date.

5. Admissions
   - current application status, opening date, deadline, entrance/interview date, required documents, application fee, application link, source URL, checked date.

6. Student support and facilities
   - only facilities/services explicitly stated by an official source (library, labs, hostel, transport, counselling, accessibility, clubs, etc.).

7. Outcomes and achievements
   - only dated, attributable, source-backed facts such as a named official result, award or event. Include source URL and date. Otherwise write unknown.

8. Editorial review flags
   - missing facts, conflicting sources, outdated pages, unverifiable claims, duplicate record risk, taxonomy correction needed, and next official source to check.

9. Source ledger
   - source title | direct URL | publisher | publication date if shown | date checked | facts supported

Use concise factual language. Do not write promotional copy.
```
